# Cognitive Partner: Pitch Deck

## Problem

High-performing professionals carry an invisible cognitive load:
- Context switching
- Decision fatigue
- Emotional labor
- Anticipatory stress

Existing tools **add** load, not reduce it. Another inbox. Another dashboard. Another system to maintain.

## Solution

**Cognitive Partner** — an AI companion that:
1. Learns your patterns and rhythms
2. Anticipates your needs before you ask
3. Adjusts support based on your energy and stress level
4. Operates in three modes: Protector (overwhelm), Navigator (planning), Anchor (emotional support)

**It's not a task manager. It's a person in your corner.**
