# Go-to-Market & Strategy

## Positioning

**Tagline:** "Your cognitive load just got lighter."

**Positioning Statement:**
Cognitive Partner is the first AI companion designed specifically for people carrying high mental load — not to optimize them, but to make space for what matters.

## Target User Personas

### 1. Sarah: The Ambitious Operator (B2C Primary)
- **Age:** 28-35
- **Profession:** Product Manager, Founder, Consultant
- **Pain:** Juggling roadmap, team needs, personal life
- **Why Us:** Wants someone who notices when she's running on empty

### 2. Marcus: The Overwhelmed Manager (B2B)
- **Age:** 35-45
- **Profession:** Mid-level manager in tech
- **Pain:** 1:1s, deadlines, emails. Constant context switching.
- **Why Us:** Needs something that takes the invisible load off

## Market Size

- **TAM:** High-stress professionals (US): ~45M
- **SAM:** Tech-forward, willing to pay: ~8M
- **SOM (Year 3):** 100k users @ $60 ARPU = $6M ARR

## Customer Acquisition

### Phase 1 (Months 1-6): Product-Led, Direct
- Free tier virality
- Content marketing (newsletter, blog, podcast)
- Direct outreach to founders/CTOs

### Phase 2 (Months 6-12): B2B2C Partnerships
- Corporate wellness programs
- HR tech integrations
- Industry verticals (healthcare, law, finance)

### Phase 3 (Months 12+): Brand Positioning
- Thought leadership
- Research publications
- Influencer partnerships

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct Competitors:** None

**Adjacent Competitors:**
- Woebot, Youper: Mental health chatbots
- Notion, Todoist: Task management
- Calendly, Motion: Calendar optimization

**Our Moat:**
- Proprietary mode transition logic
- Privacy architecture
- Warm tone + humor

## Pricing

- **Free:** $0/month (5 sessions/month)
- **Pro:** $9.99/month or $99/year
- **Enterprise:** $5-8/employee/month

## 12-Month Roadmap

**Q1:** MVP with 3 modes, onboarding, free + pro tiers
**Q2:** Calendar & Slack integration, advanced mode logic
**Q3:** Voice input/output, B2B pilot program
**Q4:** Apple Watch, mood dashboard, enterprise sales
